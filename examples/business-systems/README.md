# Business systems

ERP-type apps that run a company's operations: stock, purchasing, orders, invoices and accounting.

Planned:

- [backoffice](backoffice) (ready)
- warehouse
- accounting

## Why

- Copilot spending goes mostly to tools for a company's own staff, not to tools for its customers ([Menlo Ventures, 2025-12-09](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)).
- Finance teams use AI most for accounts payable (37%) and anomaly detection (34%) ([Gartner, 2025-11-18](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-18-gartner-survey-shows-finance-ai-adoption-remains-steady-in-2025)). That is the case for accounting.
- Warehouse has no survey data yet. It is here because stock, invoices and reports are bulk, repetitive work, which is where the agent's scripts save the most time.
