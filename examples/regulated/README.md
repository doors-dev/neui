## Why

- Finance teams use AI for accounts payable (37%) and anomaly detection (34%) ([Gartner, 2025-11-18](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-18-gartner-survey-shows-finance-ai-adoption-remains-steady-in-2025)). Morningstar, MUFG and Modern Treasury are named adopters ([LangChain customers](https://langchain.com/customers)).
