# Examples

Example apps built with Doors and neui, grouped by the audience and use case they serve. Each folder will hold 2 to 3 examples.

| Folder | Example | Function | Industry | What the agent shows | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| business-systems | [backoffice](business-systems/backoffice) | Orders, customers, products | Retail | Operates tables and forms, page tools, Workbench apps | Ready |
| business-systems | warehouse | Warehouse management | Logistics, retail | Receives supplier invoices, matches them to orders, updates stock and builds reports | Planned |
| business-systems | accounting | Payments and invoices | Any | Matches payments to invoices in bulk and prepares month-end reports | Planned |
| internal-tools | support-console | Customer support | Any | Role-scoped actions, bulk ticket updates with one script | Planned |
| internal-tools | ops-console | IT operations | Infrastructure | Runbooks and bulk actions across services | Planned |
| saas | analytics | Data analysis | Any | Agent-built report screens from your components | Planned |
| saas | merchant-admin | Store management | E-commerce | The agent runs the product for its users | Planned |
| saas | crm | Sales | Any | Lead qualification and bulk deal updates | Planned |
| regulated | clinic | Appointments, records | Healthcare | Scoped access to patient data | Planned |
| regulated | finance-approvals | Accounts payable | Finance | Approvals that only people can confirm | Planned |
| portals | account-self-service | Customer service | Any | The agent acts on the customer's own account | Planned |
| portals | claims | Claims filing | Insurance | The customer sees only their own policies | Planned |
